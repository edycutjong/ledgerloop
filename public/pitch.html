LedgerLoop

AI-orchestrated trustless rotating savings circles backed by on-chain escrow and GNN trust scoring

Introduce LedgerLoop. 1 billion unbanked rely on savings circles. We replace human organizers with smart contracts and AI trust scoring.
$1.7T

trapped in informal savings circles globally — with 30% default rates and zero credit portability

$1.7 trillion sits in ROSCAs worldwide. 30% of these circles fail due to organizer fraud or participant default. No credit history is generated.
THE PROBLEM

Maria trusted her tanda organizer.
He vanished with $4,200.

Organizer Fraud

No escrow. The organizer holds all funds and can disappear after collecting contributions.

Participant Default

After receiving their payout, members stop contributing — destroying the circle for everyone else.

Zero Credit History

Years of reliable contributions generate no credit score. The unbanked stay unbanked.

Tell Maria's story. She contributed for 6 months. The organizer took the pot and left. She has no recourse and no credit history from her contributions.
THE SOLUTION

Replace the organizer with code.

⛓️ Solidity Escrow

Deposits locked in auditable smart contracts. No human custody. ~$0.014/tx on Arbitrum.

🧠 GNN Trust Scoring

Graph Neural Network analyzes wallet transaction graphs to detect sybil loops and wash-trading clusters.

🔄 Dynamic Rotation

Trust scores determine payout order. Riskier wallets get later turns or require 50% collateral.

Three pillars: Solidity escrow holds funds, GNN scores wallet behavior, and dynamic rotation penalizes risky members with later payouts or collateral requirements.
